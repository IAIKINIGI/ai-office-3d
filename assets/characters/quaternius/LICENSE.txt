The glTF in this folder is the free Standard pack (Godot export) mirrored from
https://github.com/J-Ponzo/gltf-universal-animation-library
which redistributes the itch.io release of 2025-06-10.

Penny uses A_TPose as the source rest pose, plus Idle_Loop, Walk_Loop and
Sitting_Idle_Loop. The clips are retargeted onto her skeleton in the page.
The mannequin mesh is not shown.
