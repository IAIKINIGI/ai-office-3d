Cyberpunk Diner Sign (reusable / relettered variants)
=====================================================

A derivative of "Cyberpunk Diner Sign (Japanese)"
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cyberpunk-diner-sign-japanese-6ffce0f168534501bdd9199847d57831
by LukeMarsh201 (https://sketchfab.com/LukeMarsh201)
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The original lettering is 3D geometry with a solid-colour emissive texture.
These files keep the original frame, neon bars and sign board, replace the
lettering with a swappable text plate, and add wording/colour variants.
Credit LukeMarsh201 when you use the original or these derivatives.
