Cyberpunk Desk
==============

This work is based on "Cyberpunk Desk"
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cyberpunk-desk-8bc4ca48b2e244ff8b5ba714a2ec1963
by Cisco (https://sketchfab.com/Ciscow)
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

This file is a web-ready derivative: textures were compressed, and the
desk was recentered and scaled so the desktop sits about 1.10 m above
the floor (standing-desk height). A floating wall socket and cable are
part of the original model. Credit Cisco when you use the original or
this derivative.
