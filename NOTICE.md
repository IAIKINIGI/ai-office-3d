  The interior backdrop is original and is not a derivative of that mesh.
- This pass does **not** add “Cyberpunk Assets” by panda.vc (CC-BY-SA-4.0) or
  the hovercar (CC-BY-SA-4.0), and it does not load or copy Penny’s
  Sketchfab Standard GLB.
